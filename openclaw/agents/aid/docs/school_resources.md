# GT-like State University: money help resources

*Demo content for BidBy. "GT-like State University" is fictional; these offices, forms and amounts are made up for the demo.*

## Office of Financial Aid
- Email: finaid@gtlstate.example
- In person: Student Services Building, Room 110, Monday to Friday 9 AM to 5 PM
- Verification documents are uploaded in the Financial Aid Portal under "Required Documents".

## Tuition payment plan
Split the term balance into 4 monthly payments with a $25 enrollment fee. Enroll in the Bursar portal before the term's first payment due date. Avoids late fees and registration holds.

## Emergency grant
Up to $500 for students facing an unexpected expense (rent, medical, travel home, lost income). Apply through the Dean of Students online form; decisions within 2 business days. It does not need to be repaid.

## Financial aid appeal (special circumstances)
If your family's income dropped or changed since the tax year on your FAFSA (job loss, medical costs, divorce, death in the family), ask the aid office for a Special Circumstances Review using Form SC-1. The office can adjust your aid. Ask for an extension on verification documents at the same time if you need it.

## Short-term tuition loan
Interest-free loan up to $1,000, repaid within 90 days. Apply at the Bursar's Office.
